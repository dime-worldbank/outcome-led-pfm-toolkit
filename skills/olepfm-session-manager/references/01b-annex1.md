### Prompt 1-13 — Annex Table 1.2: Organizational Functions

Please prepare Annex Table 1.2 for [country] in the [sector] sector, consistent with the organizational landscape and diagram and PFM analysis in Chapter 2.

The table must have exactly four columns as in the OLePFM Sector Reform Design Report Template:
| Organization | Level of Government | Policy and Delivery Functions | Public Financial Management Functions |

Rules for populating the table:
- Cover all main levels in the delivery chain: Central Government | Regional/Local Government | Sub-county/Frontline/Community.
- Also include non-government actors where they play a significant role in delivery or financing.
- Policy and Delivery Functions: what does this organization do in terms of policy formulation, service planning, delivery, coordination, and oversight?
- Public Financial Management Functions: what does this organization do in terms of budgeting, resource mobilization, financial reporting, audit, procurement, and performance accountability?
- Be specific — avoid generic descriptions that could apply to any organization.

**Check that no significant actor has been omitted — including frontline service providers, community-level actors, and private sector participants — before presenting as a draft.**

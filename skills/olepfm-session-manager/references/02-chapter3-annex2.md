Using the sources identified and the OLePFM Synthesis and relevant Sector Outcome Note as a guide, and strictly following the Chapter 3 structure in the OLePFM Sector Reform Design Report Template, please draft Section 3.1 — The Role of Public Finance — for [country] in the [sector] sector.

For each of the four roles — (3.1.1) Commitment to Feasible Policy, (3.1.2) Fiscal Sustainability, (3.1.3) Strategic Resource Mobilization and Distribution, and (3.1.4) Productivity, Transparency and Accountability in Delivery — write a paragraph of approximately 150–200 words setting out:
- **Potential Role:** describe what public finance could and should do in [sector] in [country] if it were functioning well — be specific to the sector's delivery model and institutional architecture.
- **Actual Role:** describe what public finance is actually doing — where it falls short of the potential, with specific evidence; be analytically honest about the gaps.

Use the introductory text from the template for the chapter and section openings. Cite all sources as hyperlinked parentheticals, e.g. ([World Bank, 2010](https://…)).

## Sub-step 2.2 — Identify and prioritize PFM bottlenecks (Section 3.2 and Annex Tables 2.2 and 2.3)

### ✏️ Review the output — focus on these points

**Five-Why Analysis, per challenge (Prompt 2-03)** — the most analytically intensive review in Step 2. Do this after each challenge, before the next.

- [ ] The bottlenecks are the most important structural causes of the challenge — not symptoms or restatements of it
- [ ] The causal chain (why → why → why) is logically consistent and supported by evidence
- [ ] The bottlenecks are genuinely distinct from one another
- [ ] The type classification (Technical / Institutional / PFM) is correct — in particular, PFM bottlenecks genuinely relate to the management of public funds
- [ ] The impact and feasibility ratings are accurate
- [ ] The number of bottlenecks (maximum five per challenge) is appropriate

You can rename a bottleneck, merge two closely related ones, challenge a causal link, adjust an impact or feasibility rating, or add one that was missed. **Confirm the bottleneck list for each challenge before proceeding to the next.**

### Prompt 2-03 — Five-Why Bottleneck Analysis (repeat for each challenge)

*Use this prompt once for each public sector challenge identified in Section 2.2. Repeat for each challenge before moving to Prompt 2-04.*

Take Challenge [number]: [paste challenge title and description from Section 2.2].

(i) Ask "why does that challenge take place?", then for the answers you reach ask "why?" again. Repeat this up to five times, using available evidence from the sources identified. Stop when there is no further evidence.

(ii) List out all the answers you generate.

(iii) Group the reasons into related problems or bottlenecks — identify up to five bottlenecks for this challenge.

(iv) For each bottleneck, provide: a name describing the bottleneck; and a description of the nature of the bottleneck.

(v) Categorize each bottleneck as: Technical (relating to the sector); Institutional (relating to people, organizations, rules, systems and accountability that are not public finance related); or PFM-related (those related to the management of public funds).

(vi) Rate the impact on service delivery as: Very High (VH), High (H), Medium (M), or Low (L).

(vii) Rate the feasibility of implementing reforms to address the bottleneck as: High (H), Medium (M), or Low (L).

Present findings using the format of Annex Step 2 Table 2.2 from the OLePFM Sector Reform Design Report Template, with columns: Public Sector Challenge | Sector | Bottleneck | Type of Bottleneck | Impact on Service Delivery | Feasibility of Reform.

### Prompt 2-06 — Priority Ranking

Using the lens of feasibility and impact on service delivery, please rank the consolidated bottlenecks in terms of priority, with Very High impact and High feasibility being the highest priority.

Where bottlenecks share the same impact and feasibility ratings, consider which are most foundational — that is, unblocking them enables progress on others.

Present the ranked list in a table with columns: Priority | CB# | Bottleneck Name | Group | Impact | Feasibility | Rationale for Ranking.

Then present the bottlenecks organized into priority tiers (Immediate, High, Medium, Long Term) with an indicative action horizon for each tier.

**2-Q1 — Template Compliance Check**

Please check that the section headings, subheadings, and introductory texts in the draft Chapter 3 strictly follow the OLePFM Sector Reform Design Report Template. Identify any deviations and correct them.

**2-Q3 — Evidence Check**

Please check that all factual claims in Chapter 3 are supported by a hyperlinked parenthetical citation, e.g. ([World Bank, 2010](https://…)). Flag any unsupported claims and either add a citation or qualify the claim appropriately.

**2-Q4 — Word Count Check**

Please check that: (i) each role description in Section 3.1 does not exceed 200 words; (ii) each challenge description in Section 3.2.1 does not exceed 300 words; (iii) each bottleneck description in Section 3.2.2 does not exceed 200 words; and (iv) Annex Table 2.1 does not exceed 500 words in total. Identify and shorten any sections that exceed their word limit.

**2-Q5 — PFM Focus Check**

Please review the bottleneck descriptions in Sections 3.2.1 and 3.2.2 and Annex Tables 2.2 and 2.3 to ensure that: (i) bottlenecks classified as PFM-related genuinely relate to the management of public funds; (ii) bottlenecks classified as Institutional do not overlap with PFM bottlenecks; and (iii) no bottleneck is so broad as to be beyond the scope of a PFM reform programme. Flag any bottlenecks that require reclassification or rescoping and suggest revisions.
